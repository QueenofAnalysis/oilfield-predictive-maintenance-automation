# Engineering Rules

The workflow evaluates equipment health using predefined engineering thresholds.

## Critical Conditions

Temperature > 85°C

OR

Vibration > 5 mm/s

OR

Flow Rate < 400 bpd

## Normal Conditions

Temperature ≤ 85°C

Vibration ≤ 5 mm/s

Flow Rate ≥ 400 bpd

The workflow assigns a Risk Level and Failure Reason before triggering maintenance notifications.
